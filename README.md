## ECM Invariants



The Entangled Coherence Model defines the minimal structural conditions for coherence. These invariants are derived directly from the ECM manuscript, which formalizes the three lenses, their mutual constraints, and the collapse modes.



\### 1. Lens Invariants

ECM identifies three irreducible interpretive lenses:



\- \*\*Physics Lens (Empirical Constraint)\*\* — empirical adequacy, measurement, causal structure, predictive reliability.

\- \*\*Metaphysics Lens (Ontological Constraint)\*\* — coherence, identity, modality, structural intelligibility.

\- \*\*Existential Lens (Axiological Constraint)\*\* — meaning, value, purpose, orientation.



These lenses are \*\*irreducible and mutually constraining\*\*.



\### 2. Coherence Invariant

A system is coherent if and only if all three lenses are satisfied:



\*\*C(x) ↔ P(x) ∧ M(x) ∧ E(x)\*\*



\### 3. Collapse Invariants

Each collapse mode is defined by exclusive satisfaction of a single lens:



\- \*\*Reductionism\*\* — P only

\- \*\*Abstraction Drift\*\* — M only

\- \*\*Existential Collapse\*\* — E only



\### 4. Domain Completeness

The three lenses exhaust the interpretive space.



\### 5. Non‑Hierarchy

No lens is privileged over another.
